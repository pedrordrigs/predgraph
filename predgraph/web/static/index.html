Hit % counts only responses that moved materially and went the way the signed path predicted; sub-threshold moves are excluded because their sign is a coin flip and including them drags every cohort toward 50%. The control cohort is unconnected markets with a random predicted sign and should sit near 50% — connected cohorts only mean something if they beat it. corr 4h relates predicted effect size to realized move, which sign agreement alone cannot test. t½ is the median hours to complete half the eventual 48h move: the size of the window a trade would have.
